<div align="center">
  <h2>💡 The Problem</h2>
</div>

Every day, hundreds of open-source projects are born. Most will die in obscurity. A handful will reshape entire industries — Kubernetes, Next.js, Tailwind, Bun, Drizzle — tools that were once invisible until suddenly they weren't.

**You discover them too late.** You read about them in blog posts written six months after the tipping point. You adopt them after your competitors already shipped with them. You're always reacting. Never anticipating.

The noise-to-signal ratio in open source is **catastrophic**. And nobody is solving it.

<br/>

<div align="center">
  <h2>🔮 The Solution</h2>
</div>

**Openlysts doesn't aggregate. It anticipates.**

We built an autonomous intelligence engine that continuously scans the entire open-source landscape — **tens of thousands of repositories** across every major category — and surfaces the ones exhibiting structural breakout patterns. Not trending. Not popular. *Breaking out.*

| | What we track | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ | **Star velocity** — not count, *acceleration* | A repo gaining stars per week matters more than one sitting at 50k |
| 🧬 | **Community metabolism** — issue resolution, PR merge rate | Dead repos have stars too. Living repos have momentum |
| 🔄 | **SaaS displacement signal** — OSS alternatives to paid tools | The SaaS market is being unbundled in real-time. We show you what's replacing what |
| 🎯 | **Category intelligence** — hundreds of curated verticals | From AI/ML to DevOps to Databases — structured, not scattered |

<br/>

<div align="center">
  <h2>⚙️ Under the Hood</h2>
</div>

- **Sub-millisecond resolution** — The entire catalog resolves instantly. No spinners. No loading screens. Just answers.
- **Zero-dependency search** — The full ranked catalog loads into memory on boot. Your queries never touch a slow database.
- **Algorithmic ranking** — Stars are vanity. We score by velocity, freshness, community health, and displacement potential.
- **Autonomous ingestion** — A background pipeline continuously re-crawls, re-ranks, and re-categorizes the full catalog. It never sleeps.
