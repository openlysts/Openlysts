## ⚡ What is this?

Most developers find tools *after* they've exploded in popularity. They scroll through noisy feeds, read biased articles, and miss the actual structural shifts happening in open-source.

**Openlysts is a hyper-optimized intelligence engine.** We autonomously track, rank, and categorize over **49,000+** open-source systems in real-time. We analyze velocity, community health, and algorithmic momentum to surface the exact tools you need, exactly when you need them.

### 🌌 The Architecture of Discovery

- **Sub-Millisecond Engine**: Our proprietary in-memory catalog resolves thousands of repositories instantly, without ever hitting a database.
- **Algorithmic Momentum**: We don't just count stars. We measure the velocity of adoption, issue resolution rates, and developer engagement to detect true algorithmic momentum.
- **SaaS-to-OSS Matrix**: Instantly pivot from expensive SaaS products to self-hosted, sovereign open-source alternatives.
- **The Intelligence Feed**: A continuously updating, live-ranked stream of the global open-source ecosystem.
