## 1. Parties
This Master Service Agreement ("MSA") is between the entity or individual using the Service ("Customer") and the Openlysts project ("Provider"). By creating an account or using the Service, Customer agrees to this MSA and the incorporated documents: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, and Acceptable Use Policy.

## 2. Definitions
- **Service** — the Openlysts web application and API (openlysts.dpdns.org), including the open-source discovery engine, comparisons, collections, bookmarks, and developer profile features.
- **Customer Data** — all content, profile information, preferences, bookmarks, and other data Customer submits to the Service.
- **Free Tier** — the no-cost usage plan. **Business/Enterprise tiers** — paid plans offered from time to time.
- **License** — the Service is proprietary and closed-source; its license permits use of the hosted application only (see `LICENSE` in the repository).

## 3. Provision of Service
3.1 Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Service during the term, subject to this MSA.
3.2 The Service is provided **"as is"** on a best-effort basis. Free-tier Service has no uptime SLA; paid tiers will define an SLA in a separate addendum.
3.3 Provider may perform scheduled maintenance; reasonable notice will be given where practicable.
3.4 Provider may suspend access immediately if: (a) Customer breaches this MSA, (b) continued operation risks the security or stability of the Service or other customers, or (c) required by law.

## 4. Customer Obligations
4.1 Customer is responsible for all activity under its account(s), including safeguarding credentials.
4.2 Customer must not: reverse-engineer the hosted Service to extract the private catalog algorithms, scrape the Service at abusive volumes, attempt unauthorized access, upload malicious code, or use the Service to violate law.
4.3 Customer must be at least 18 years old and provide accurate registration data.

## 5. Customer Data & Privacy
5.1 Customer retains all rights to Customer Data. Customer grants Provider a limited license to host, process, and display Customer Data solely to operate the Service.
5.2 Provider processes Customer Data per the Privacy Policy and applicable law (including, for users in India, the DPDP Act 2023; for users in the EU/UK, the GDPR).
5.3 On account deletion, Customer Data is erased (hard-delete) within a reasonable period per the Privacy Policy.

## 6. Fees, Taxes, and Payment
6.1 Free Tier: no fees. Provider may introduce or change pricing for paid tiers with at least 30 days' notice.
6.2 Paid tiers: fees as set out in the applicable order form or plan page, due in advance; taxes added where required.

## 7. Intellectual Property
7.1 The Service (excluding Customer Data and third-party components under their own licenses) is owned by Provider and licensed under the proprietary license published in the repository, which grants use of the hosted application only.
7.2 Nothing in this MSA transfers ownership of Provider IP. Customer may not remove license or attribution notices.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each party must protect the other's non-public business and technical information with at least reasonable care, and use it only to perform this MSA. This does not apply to information that is public, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 9. Term & Termination
9.1 Term: month-to-month unless otherwise agreed; free-tier accounts continue until deleted.
9.2 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 15 days of notice.
9.3 On termination, Customer may export its data for 30 days ("data portability window"); after that window Customer Data is deleted.

## 10. Warranties & Disclaimers
10.1 The Service is provided "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement.
10.2 Provider does not warrant that catalog data is error-free, complete, or continuously available; repository data originates from third-party public sources (e.g., GitHub) and is provided for informational purposes.

## 11. Limitation of Liability
11.1 To the maximum extent permitted by law, Provider's aggregate liability for all claims arising out of or related to this MSA shall not exceed the greater of (a) fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim, or (b) USD 100.
11.2 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, revenue, data, or goodwill.

## 12. Indemnification
Customer will indemnify and hold Provider harmless from claims arising from Customer's breach of this MSA, misuse of the Service, or violation of law, except to the extent caused by Provider's negligence.

## 13. Data Protection & Security
13.1 Provider applies industry-standard safeguards: TLS in transit, hashed password storage (bcrypt), hashed verification tokens, session security, rate limiting, bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile), and security audit logging.
13.2 Provider will notify Customer without undue delay of a personal-data breach affecting Customer Data where required by law.

## 14. Compliance & Export
Customer agrees to comply with all applicable export control and sanctions laws. The Service may not be used in sanctioned jurisdictions.
